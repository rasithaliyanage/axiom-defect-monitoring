Business Requirements Document (BRD)
AI-Based Automated Board Defect Inspection System
Document Type: Business Requirements DocumentProject: AI-Based Board Defect DetectionPrepared By: Business Analysis / AI Discovery TeamAudience: Business Stakeholders, Manufacturing Team, Quality Team, Solution Architects, AI/ML Team, Engineering Team, Data TeamStatus: Discovery / Business RequirementsVersion: 1.0

1. Executive Summary
The manufacturing organization currently performs quality inspection of boards produced on a manufacturing line. The organization intends to investigate and implement an AI-assisted visual inspection capability using cameras to automatically identify defective boards.
The proposed initiative will use images captured from the manufacturing process and apply AI-based analysis to determine whether a board meets the required quality standards.
The business objective is not simply to introduce AI, but to improve the accuracy, consistency, speed, scalability, and traceability of board inspection while maintaining or improving the existing quality standards.
The proposed capability should be able to:
Capture images of boards at defined inspection points. 
Analyze captured images. 
Determine whether a board is acceptable or defective. 
Identify predefined defect types. 
Identify the location of a defect where required. 
Provide confidence information for AI predictions. 
Support human review where the AI prediction is uncertain. 
Record inspection results for traceability and reporting. 
Integrate with relevant manufacturing/quality systems where required. 
The exact AI/ML technique, camera technology, deployment architecture, infrastructure, and implementation approach are not defined by this BRD and should be determined during the solution-design and AI feasibility phases.

2. Business Problem
2.1 Problem Statement
The organization requires an improved method of identifying defective boards during manufacturing.
The current inspection process may involve manual inspection and/or existing automated inspection mechanisms. The organization is exploring AI-based visual inspection to determine whether defects can be identified more consistently and efficiently.
The core business problem is:
How can the organization reliably identify defective boards during production using visual information while meeting required quality, production throughput, and operational requirements?

3. Business Opportunity
The introduction of AI-based visual inspection may provide opportunities to:
Detect defects earlier in the manufacturing process. 
Reduce defects escaping inspection. 
Improve inspection consistency. 
Reduce dependence on manual inspection. 
Increase inspection throughput. 
Improve inspection traceability. 
Provide objective inspection evidence. 
Reduce rework and scrap. 
Create data that can be used for quality analysis. 
Establish a foundation for future intelligent manufacturing capabilities. 
The business case should ultimately determine whether the expected benefits justify the investment.

4. Business Objectives
The initiative should pursue the following objectives.
BO-01 — Automated Inspection
Provide an automated or AI-assisted mechanism for visually inspecting boards.
BO-02 — Defect Detection
Identify predefined board defects during production.
BO-03 — Quality Improvement
Reduce the probability of defective boards passing through the inspection process.
BO-04 — Inspection Consistency
Reduce variability associated with manual visual inspection.
BO-05 — Production Efficiency
Perform inspection within the required production-line throughput.
BO-06 — Traceability
Maintain inspection results and supporting evidence for quality investigation.
BO-07 — Human Decision Support
Allow human inspectors to review uncertain or disputed AI decisions where required.
BO-08 — Continuous Improvement
Establish a mechanism through which inspection results and human feedback can contribute to future model improvement.

15. Functional Business Requirements
FR-001 — Board Image Capture
The system shall support inspection using images captured from boards at designated production inspection points.
FR-002 — Board Identification
The inspection process shall associate an inspection result with the relevant board identifier where such an identifier exists.
FR-003 — Defect Detection
The system shall identify predefined defect categories within the supported inspection scope.
FR-004 — Board Classification
The system shall determine whether the inspected board meets the defined quality criteria.
Expected logical output:
PASS
FAIL
REVIEW
where applicable.
FR-005 — Defect Classification
For defective boards, the system shall identify the applicable defect category where classification is required.
FR-006 — Defect Localization
The system shall identify the location of the defect where required by the business.
FR-007 — Confidence
The system shall provide a confidence measure associated with an AI prediction where supported by the selected solution.
FR-008 — Multiple Defects
The system shall support identification of multiple defects on the same board where required.
Example:
Board 123
   │
   ├── Missing Component — 98%
   ├── Solder Bridge — 96%
   └── Misalignment — 91%
FR-009 — Human Review
The system shall support human review of results that meet defined review conditions.
FR-010 — Human Override
Authorized personnel shall be able to override an AI decision where business rules permit.
FR-011 — Inspection Evidence
The system shall retain inspection evidence required for quality investigation, subject to data-retention requirements.
FR-012 — Inspection History
The system shall allow authorized users to access historical inspection results.

16. AI Requirements

The AI capability shall achieve agreed detection performance for each in-scope defect category.
AI-002 — Critical Defects

AI-003 — False Negatives

AI-005 — Confidence Thresholds
The solution shall support appropriate handling of low-confidence predictions.
AI-006 — Model Version
The model version associated with an inspection result shall be identifiable.
AI-007 — Model Validation
Models shall be validated against an independent test dataset before production use.
